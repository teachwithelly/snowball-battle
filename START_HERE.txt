SNOWBALL BATTLE — HOW TO PLAY

1. Unzip the entire Snowball_Battle_Game.zip archive.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
3. Choose Elf Girl or Elf Boy and press START GAME.
4. Move the mouse over the lower snowfield to move your elf.
5. Answer each question. After a correct answer, CLICK a highlighted opponent to throw.
6. After a wrong answer, the enemy throws and you lose one heart.
7. I DON'T KNOW / SKIP moves that question to the back of the queue without losing a heart.
8. Teacher's Mode lets you edit exactly 10 questions, select Quiz or Speaking, and export/import JSON.
9. To publish online, upload the entire folder (including assets/) to GitHub Pages.

NOTES:
- Player Boy's original archive lacks a reliably identifiable throw sequence; his idle pose is used during throws.
- Sprite sizes were normalized from the provided images. The originals remain in the source subfolders.
- The game is designed for desktop mouse use; pointer/touch may work on tablets but has not been device-tested.
- Questions are saved locally in the browser; export JSON to share or back up.
